Limitations

  • Type detection uses a limited table of common file signatures; unknown types are reported as unknown, not as suspicious.
  • Modification times come from the file system of the examined copy and may differ from the original source.
  • A result without findings does not prove that a file is authentic or unaltered before acquisition.
  • A trusted timestamp proves the audit log existed in this exact form at the stated time; it does not prove that the examined copy matches the original source.
  • The absence of findings does not prove that a PDF is authentic or unaltered: a document can be forged before it is saved, or re-created so that no trace remains.
  • When a PDF is printed to PDF, flattened, optimised or regenerated, its earlier revisions and most editing traces are lost; only its last state can be examined.
  • Producer fingerprints come from a limited database (vendor documentation and files generated by Rakvor); producer strings can be typed by hand, and unknown or spoofed values cannot be recognised.
  • Digital signatures are checked for byte coverage and cryptographic integrity only; certificate chains, revocation and the signer's identity are not validated.
  • The visual comparison between revisions renders only a limited number of pages per revision; pages beyond that limit are compared structurally only.
