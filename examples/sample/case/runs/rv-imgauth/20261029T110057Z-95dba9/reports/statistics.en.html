Limitations

  • An image without findings is not proven authentic: careful edits can leave none of the traces these checks look for, and it is impossible to prove a negative (SWGDE 18-I-001).
  • Error level, noise and ghost maps are visual aids for a trained examiner; the tool gives them no score and draws no conclusion from them.
  • Double JPEG compression shows the image was saved more than once; ordinary sharing and exporting do this.
  • The compression-table database covers few devices and apps (built from Rakvor's own captures plus local learning); an absent or matching entry is weak evidence on its own.
  • Copy-move detection compares blocks at the same scale and orientation on a downscaled image: scaled, rotated or small copies are not found.
  • Error rates of these checks have not yet been measured on a documented validation corpus; the findings are signals to be weighed by the examiner.
  • Modification times come from the file system of the examined copy and may differ from the original source.
  • A trusted timestamp proves the audit log existed in this exact form at the stated time; it does not prove that the examined copy matches the original source.
  • Type detection uses a limited table of common file signatures; unknown types are reported as unknown, not as suspicious.
